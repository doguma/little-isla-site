Privacy Policy for Little Isla

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Little Isla is designed as a private, on-device voice companion. We do not operate a backend server for conversations, user accounts, analytics, or advertising.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Conversations, Memories, and Summaries: Stored locally on your iPhone in the app's Application Support directory (little-isla-data.json) with iOS complete file protection and backup exclusion requested.
  • Preferences & Purchase Status: Your selected theme, voice style, model choice, and offline entitlement cache for the one-time unlock (Unlock Little Isla) are stored locally on your device.
  • Deleting Your Data: You can clear chat history, delete individual memories or summaries in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

2. Speech Recognition & Voice

  • Microphone & Speech Recognition: Little Isla uses Apple's on-device Speech Recognition (requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true). Your voice audio is processed locally on your iPhone and is not uploaded by Little Isla to any server.
  • Voice Output: Isla's pixel voices are synthesized procedurally on-device. If you optionally switch to iOS System TTS, speech synthesis is handled locally by iOS.

3. On-Device AI Models & Network Access

  • Initial Model Download: On first setup (or when switching models), the app downloads public open-weight MLX model files from Hugging Face over HTTPS. No personal conversations, microphone audio, or memories are transmitted during model downloads.
  • Offline Operation: After a model is downloaded, all AI inference runs locally on your iPhone with no external AI API calls.

4. In-App Purchases

One-time purchases (Unlock Little Isla) and restores are processed directly by Apple via StoreKit. Little Isla does not collect or store your payment information or Apple Account details.

5. Third-Party Tracking & Analytics

Little Isla does not use third-party advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or cross-app tracking.
