Frequently Asked Questions

> Why does first-time setup download a model?

Little Isla runs open-weight AI models directly on your iPhone using MLX so your conversations never leave your device. Qwen3 1.7B (~1.0 GB) is included free, and Qwen2.5 3B (~1.8 GB) and Qwen3 4B (~2.3 GB) are available with Unlock Little Isla. Wi-Fi is recommended for the one-time download.

> Speech recognition says offline English isn't available

Little Isla uses Apple's on-device Speech Recognition and never falls back to cloud servers. Make sure English (U.S.) dictation/speech is enabled in iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Enable Dictation.

> How do I restore Unlock Little Isla?

Long-press Isla (or tap Settings) and select > Restore Purchases. Family Sharing is supported.

> How do I delete my local data?

Open Settings to clear chat history or delete individual memories and summaries, or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.
